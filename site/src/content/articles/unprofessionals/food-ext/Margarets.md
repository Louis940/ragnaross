# Margaret's

![Margaret's](/assets/images/food-ext/margarets.jpg)

## Why I like it:

What a place, definitely a great choice for a quality meal for an anniversary or similar. Also seems like everyone raves about their Sunday Roast. Margaret's brings the Restaurant Twenty Two quality at a fraction of the price. It probably won't get a star like its big sister but I can definitely see it getting a Bib Gourmand at some point. Great small menu of food and some delicious wine choices, with top notch service from the FoH team to boot.
