#### BunX/Micawbers Tavern

Very tasty burgers in this quality Norwich spot, and good chicken options too. Offered us a table despite the lack of a reservation (under condition of getting out of there before they needed it back). They also offered us some free burger patties for our dogs. This is the only place I've had this happen so definitely stands out. Top notch customer service.
