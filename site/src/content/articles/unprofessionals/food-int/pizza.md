As a man in my early thirties, and a father, it is apt time to choose a specialisation. Whilst I've seen friends splash hundreds or even thousands on barbecues (which I definitely want to, alas I cannot afford), I've chosen to put a bit more time into Pizza making.

A cheap birthday present from my wife and child, a secondhand Ooni Fyra picked up from St Albans in February (for a modest £75) has become a foerce tool in my arsenal. Sure it doesn't look new, rusted, burnt in places and in need of a good clean out (and from the cleaning process, in need of a new pizza stone, oops) but I was soon using it to hone the noble art of Pizza craft.

Following the recipe for Naepolitan pizza dough [off the Gozney website](https://www.gozney.com/blogs/recipes/neapolitan-pizza) involving a poolish, and a 2-3 day process I have since branched out into using sourdough (which also required learning about birthing and maintaining sourdough starters), which is still a 24 hour + process, but it cuts the time down significantly, and also emulates how some of my favourite local pizzerias do things.
