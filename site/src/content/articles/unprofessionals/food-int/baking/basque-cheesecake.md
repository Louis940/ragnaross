My solid recipe, and go to easy dessert if you want to impress.

Lots of soft cheese, double cream, eggs and sugar - it's hard to go wrong. Based mainly off a Guardian article by Felicity Cloake which took a look at multiple other recipes. A few tweaks on my part just to make it my own and not plagiarise.

### Ingredients

- 700g soft cheese (personally a fan of Asda own brand as it's cheap, but any will do - just look for high fat content)

- 350g double cream

- 300g sugar (I always use golden caster)

- 5 eggs

- 2 tablespoons of cornflour

- as much vanilla extract as you like (I use Vanilla Bean Paste for this as I prefer the taste)

### Method

1. Preheat your oven to as hot as you can, and prepare a cake tin with two layers of baking parchment.

2. Mix the cheese and sugar until smooth and creamy, then add the cream.

3. Add in the eggs one by one, waiting until each egg is fully incorporated to put in the next. Add the vanilla around this time too,

4. In a separate bowl, mix a ladle of the mixture with the cornflour to create a smooth paste. You may need a small whisk to help break up any lumps. Once smoothm add back to the main mixture and mix well.

5. Pour the mixture into the prepared tin. You may need to tap the tin on the counter to remove air bubbles.

6. Put it in the oven and reduce the heat to 180C for 40 mins to an hour. I like the top on mine to be very very brown, verging on blackened. It's a burnt cheesecake, afterall.

7. Once cooked, leave to cool in the tin for half an hour or so, then refrigerate if you like it chilled, and a bit firmer. I'm perfectly happy to server it straight from the oven, but that's also personal preference.


### Suggestions and variations

 - **Strawerries** - Make sure to make a strawberry reduction instead of just a puree in order to keep the level of liquid low. If you don't think it's red enough then add a small drop of pro-grade food colouring (something like Sugarflair here in the UK).

 - **Pistachio** - Everyone's into pistachio nowadays. I'd say get some already shelled pistachios (no matter the extra cost - I've spent so many hours of an evening just shelling pistachios, and the time saved is worth the money). Blend/Process them, you can add white chocolate if you like things sweet.

 - **Chocolate** - I like to use a 70 or 80% dark single origin for this. Melt the chocolate in with your cream, before adding to the cream cheese and sugar mix once cooled. Make sure to adjust your sugar quantity if you use a sweeter choclate.
